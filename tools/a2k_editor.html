Apple II — Joy-Map .a2k Editor

Assign Apple II keys to joystick buttons for the MiSTer core. Pick a gamepad button, then click a key. Save a 16-byte .a2k and load it on the core via OSD → System & BIOS → "Load Joy Map".

Click a button to select it (highlighted ■), then click a key on the right to assign. extra1 and extra2 are additional buttons mapped to SPACE and ENTER in the virtual keyboard; when the keyboard is off we can use them as extra buttons with a different mapping. They are blank by default.
